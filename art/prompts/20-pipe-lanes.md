Art style for TransportPipes (a cozy multiplayer industrial idle game seen in isometric view): polished, stylised,
hand-painted game art like a premium mobile tycoon game. Chunky, readable shapes; clean dark outlines (medium weight);
warm, saturated but not neon colours; soft painterly shading with gentle ambient occlusion; light from the top left
with soft shadows to the bottom right. Cheerful, cozy industry on green grass. No text, labels, logos or UI.

The idea to show: the game's chunky grey pipe is a big utility conduit that carries every way of transport inside it.
Where the pipe connects to a building, the right kind of small connector pops out of the side of the big pipe through
a brass-rimmed hatch:
- energy: two or three insulated copper power cables on tiny wooden posts, with small yellow sparks of energy travelling
  along them;
- solid goods (ore, coal, ingots, crates): a short, small conveyor belt on little legs with goods riding on it;
- fluids (water, oil): a slim pipe of its own, much thinner than the big pipe, with a little red valve wheel.
The big pipe itself looks like today's: a chunky grey tube on concrete feet with brass collars and small glowing pellets.
Keep every connector short (about one tile long) and clearly smaller than the big pipe and the building.
