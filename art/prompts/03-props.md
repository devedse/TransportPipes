STYLE
Art style for TransportPipes (a cozy multiplayer industrial idle game seen in isometric view): polished, stylised,
hand-painted game art like a premium mobile tycoon game. Chunky, readable shapes; clean dark outlines (medium weight);
warm, saturated but not neon colours; soft painterly shading with gentle ambient occlusion; light from the top left
with soft shadows to the bottom right and a subtle rim light; cheerful, lively, cozy-industrial mood (brick, painted
steel, brass, timber, glowing windows). Detailed, but every object reads clearly at small sizes. No text or logos
unless asked.

Rules for every prop image (they are aligned by a script, so follow them exactly):
- Square 1024×1024 PNG with a transparent background, the object centred with a small margin.
- The standard isometric game camera (2:1 dimetric), light from the top left, the same style for all.
- A 'prop' stands on the ground: draw only the object (with at most a tiny patch of ground at its foot), its foot at
  the bottom middle of the picture.
- A 'decal' lies flat on the ground: draw it as seen from the isometric angle, spread over a diamond twice as wide as
  it is tall, with no ground plate, no outline around it, soft edges that blend into any ground.
- No cast shadows, no people, no text.
