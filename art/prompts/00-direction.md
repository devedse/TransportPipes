Art style for TransportPipes (a cozy multiplayer industrial idle game seen in isometric view): polished, stylised,

The game: you own a valley (an "estate", 32×32 tiles) in a district around a hub city. You build energy, mines and
factories, connect them with pipes on the ground through which resources visibly flow as coloured dots, and sell to
the hub or to your neighbours. The isometric view is the standard 2:1 dimetric game angle.
