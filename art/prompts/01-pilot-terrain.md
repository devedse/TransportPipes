STYLE
Art style for TransportPipes (a cozy multiplayer industrial idle game seen in isometric view): polished, stylised,
hand-painted game art like a premium mobile tycoon game. Chunky, readable shapes; clean dark outlines (medium weight);
warm, saturated but not neon colours; soft painterly shading with gentle ambient occlusion; light from the top left
with soft shadows to the bottom right and a subtle rim light; cheerful, lively, cozy-industrial mood (brick, painted
steel, brass, timber, glowing windows). Detailed, but every object reads clearly at small sizes. No text or logos
unless asked.

Rules for every terrain texture:
- Square 1024×1024 PNG that fills the whole image (no transparency), seen straight from above (top-down, no
  perspective, no horizon, no objects that cast long shadows).
- It must tile seamlessly: what leaves one edge continues on the opposite edge; no vignette, no border, even lighting.
- Painted in the game's style: soft, slightly stylised, with small details (grass blades, pebbles, ripples), not a
  photo. Medium contrast so buildings stand out on it.

The textures:
- grass.png: lush green meadow grass with small variations in tone, tiny clover and a few tiny flowers
- water.png: clear blue lake water with soft painted ripples and light reflections, deeper blue towards no edge
- beach.png: fine golden sand with soft ripples and a few tiny shells and pebbles

Save every PNG into the current working directory with exactly the given file name. Make each image separately. Do not write any code.
