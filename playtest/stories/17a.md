# The story: §17a and §17b over five weeks

The calendar of docs/playtest-design.html §4. The director moves the clock between rows (`director.mjs advance`,
sampling every 6 hours), resumes it for a session and pauses it after. Players in one row play at the same time.
Budgets are commands (looks included): about three a minute.
