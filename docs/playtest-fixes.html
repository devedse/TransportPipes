TransportPipes · v1.2 · after the first AI playtest

Fixing Playing Together

The playtest found a lovely first evening, then five weeks side by side but never together. A panel of five, each with one point of view, read every diary, log and screenshot and checked the code. Over three rounds they decided what to change, how, in what order, and how we'll know it worked. This page is the decision.

Make it true, then show itThe Yard pays before any screen shows trade. Dead ends explain themselves before the advice reaches everyone. The project can be reached before it's put on a row.
The cause in five words, where they lookOn the building panel, the return card, Loady and the "This week" row: the four places every player read. Nothing new to find.
Give the friends something to giveAn honest Yard trades little between kits that all make energy. So the project and chains fed by a neighbour carry week 1, and every gift is announced.

1 · What the playtest showed

Four causes, in order. Everything else hangs off them.

1. The Yard took goods and paid almost nobody Measure counted every input of a slowed building as lacking at its full unused need, because the solver gives each input exactly what the building's rate uses, so they always tie. Cleo's level-35 Lab was matched 361 energy a minute from Ana, Ben and Dev and could use 0.6: about 3.5M gold vanished in eight days. A Glassworks House that could never run took a quarter of Mia's energy for 36 days. In 36 days no Yard trade paid a player.
2. Dead ends blamed their inputs Ana built her factory exactly as Loady said, piped to the mine and a windmill. Goods never pass through a building, so its ingots couldn't leave. The network card said "Energy 20 short", and she, Cleo and Dev chased energy for 32 days. Dev's dredger sat 11 days the same way. 5 of the 9 real STUCK notes.
3. Nothing to give, and a project out of reach Every starter kit makes energy, so no friend needed what a friend made. The one shared buyer, the project, asked for sand, wood and stone, which nobody made, at sizes from the balance bot's income table: 3–18 times what these players earned. The Water Tower wanted 91.5k sand and sat at 0 from day 3 to day 36. Every stage also had an 18-hour tail.
4. The screens they read carried none of it One line hid the Next steps from 4 of 5 players for 36 days. Loady stalled for weeks. Three dialogs stood before every return, without the friends' news. The "This week" row said "A new parcel: Level 5" for a month. The crew chip was clipped off the screen. Ana never learned of Ben's stand-in.

2 · How we decided

Five agents, each with one seat:

  • The systems engineer: root causes in the code, file and line, and the smallest correct fix.
  • The economy designer: every number, with worked checks against the run.
  • The clarity designer: what players see, with exact texts.
  • The cooperation designer: playing together on purpose.
  • The player researcher: the evidence, the devil's advocate, and what counts as success.

They worked in three rounds: each alone; everyone reading everyone; then the moderator's draft plan, judged item by item and voted on. The record is in docs/design-notes/playtest-fixes/.

Where they changed their minds
  • The engineer dropped an extra solve per buyer (too slow for big networks). It also took back its own seller-side floor, which would have paid project, Ask and trunk deliveries twice.
  • The economy designer withdrew the keystone valve, hub research points and the one-tap Invest.
  • The cooperation designer withdrew the crew chat channel: the crew's talk in the district chat was Mia's only sign of life. It also gave up the Loady Yard step.
  • The researcher corrected two of its own claims; the Lab's 15 points at 3% is correct arithmetic.

3 · Rules for every fix

  • One truth per fact. One IdleReason for the panel, the card, the next steps and Loady. Paid figures only, never matched ones, wherever income or trade shows.
  • The cause in the first five words. Ben skims, Dev doesn't read. The building panel shows a block only when it has something true to say, in a fixed order:
    1. the status and reach line;
    2. the one fix button;
    3. the upgrade (it stays put under the pointer);
    4. where it goes;
    5. needs and makes;
    6. its network.
  • An event budget. A toast is for what happens while you watch. What happened while you were away goes on the return card, and the bell is the archive. On a phone, one toast at a time.
  • Privacy. A trade pair is shown only to its two ends.
  • No gold moves between players, and no gold buys a shared decision. No gifts, point trading or keystone valve.
  • Keep v1.2's decisions: one price between players, Houses labelled and capped at 25%, no votes or kicks. The one exception is the price's level, which the bots decide (section 5).
  • Snapshots stay loadable. No migration is needed (companies, projects and crews are JSON), so the run's snapshots replay every fix.

4 · The fixes, in ship order

S ≤ 2 h, M ≤ a day, L more. Each step is a playable commit on the branch, and each must pass its gate (section 6) before the next ships.

F0

Instruments and replays

S–M

What it measures:

  • gold the Yard paid, beside what it matched, and what nobody took, per seller;
  • per building, idle since when, why, and whether its owner was shown why;
  • project deliveries per good and company;
  • return cards and clicks, and toasts on a phone screen;
  • the share of output traded between humans;
  • new gold from NPCs;
  • tends, thanks and the first gift.

The tools: a project restage cheat, and scripted replays from the snapshots (row01, row07, row08, row10, row14, and Dev's day 10). Every replay must first fail on today's build exactly as run 1 did.

F1

The Yard is honest

S + M
  1. The floor (S, the first commit). What the Yard matched and the buyer didn't take is paid to the seller at its own hub price.
    • It is settled on the buyer's side, for Yard matches only, capped at the seller's hub-bound spare.
    • It is booked as a hub sale, in the seller's Hub Order too.
    • A player buyer pays the premium (1.75 × base − the seller's hub price) for what it takes.
    • The project, Asks and trunks pay for their own goods as today, never twice.
  2. Lacks only what a building can use. lack ≤ need × min(f_out, f_other) − got.
    • f_out is 0 when the building is stuck, its run fraction when its own Storage or post is full, and 1 otherwise.
    • f_other covers its other inputs on its own links, plus the station's spare through its gate link.
    The IdleReason engine (game side) moves into this step. A probe solve checks the formula in tests only.
  3. Feedback. A buyer that took under 80% is next matched at most 1.25 × what it took, plus 0.5 a minute.
  4. Spare is real. It counts storage-bound output beyond what the Storage keeps, and the idle capacity of a building held back by its output.
  5. No dead Houses. The "neighbour in need" Houses get a trading post. Their missing good is one the station's humans make, chosen again daily.
  6. Screens show what was paid, last: the top bar, the Yard panel and Loady's neighbour step.

Checked against the run: Ana 33.5 + 194 ≈ 228 g/min through the Lab week (her real rate after it: 228–231). The Lab's energy lack is capped at 0.1 a minute.

F2

Dead ends explain themselves

M

IdleReason is decided from links, never from this round's flows, so it can't flicker. It distinguishes stuck, taker stuck, full ("Slowed: its Storage is full"), input missing, input short, post full, pumps and damaged.

The network card no longer counts a stuck building as "short", and the advisor's shortage steps skip it.

The panel:

  • the pill reads ⛔ Stuck: ingots can't leave;
  • the reach line reads Its pipes reach only: Iron Mine, Wind Moily.;
  • one button, Lay the pipe to your Trading Post · 12 g, planned with the player's own pipe rules and laid on a click;
  • when the best taker is the project or a neighbour, the button goes to the Yard gate instead: The Water Tower wants its sand at your Yard gate (G) · Lay the pipe to your Yard gate · 26 tiles · 52 g.

Elsewhere: a map bubble on a stuck building and a goal-tracker alert for everyone. Loady's factory step reads "build it right on the pipe from your Iron Mine to your Trading Post … Goods never pass through a building." Its Show me places the ghost on that pipe.

F3

Next steps for everyone, Loady follows the estate

S–M · with F2
  • The Next steps show from Level 3 in every chapter, collapsed on a phone. They ship with F2, so the advice they spread is true.
  • The starter kit counts from step 3 on. Step 2 stays the player's own first pipe, the lesson Ben needed.
  • The kit's own gate pipe still completes Loady's Yard step, whose text teaches the door: "Your kit is piped to your Yard gate (G): that's your door to the neighbours. What reaches it by pipe goes to their buildings, and to the Water Tower, by itself."
  • A step open for two play days shrinks to one line with its tip. Chapter 1 retires at Level 10 for everyone.
  • No levels in step texts, no duplicate titles, and nothing the player demolished in the last 7 days is suggested.
  • The advisor's effort term becomes max(200, 10 minutes of income, 2% of the bank), so with a big bank the steps rank by what they bring. For Cleo on day 14, "moily to 30 + post to 10" (+1,760 g/min for 114k) ranks first instead of "+16 for 353 g".
F4

A project the station can reach

L
Foundation900 energy. It stands about 20:12 for the crew and 20:21 for Mia alone on kits (pass: 20:30).
No tailThe project takes all that's offered, up to what's left, in proportion. A good is done at 99.5% or under 1 unit. 150 ore takes about 10 minutes, not 19 hours.
RecipeTwo goods active residents make now, or can make with one building unlocked within the stage's horizon (Water Tower Level 5, Workshop 7, Foundry Hall and after 10), preferring goods made now and goods other than energy. "Makes" means running output that reaches a gate.
KeystoneA good nobody makes yet that an active resident can make with one building, on land they have or can buy. It is picked again only when no active resident can make it, never while someone delivers it. There's no keystone when none qualifies. It is exempt from the allowance, and is 6 h of one level-1 building (sand 5,400, stone 7,200).
SizeMade goods are 4 h / 6 h / 9 h of the station's spare (at least that much of one level-1 building). The whole basket is at most 1.5 × the stage's hours of summed measured income. Baskets only shrink: a counted resident's part comes off after 3 days without a session. The crew's Water Tower: about 93k instead of 329k, done around day 3.
Measured incomeI(c) is a 24-h average of production gold a minute (at least 60). It replaces the bot's table for allowances (2 h a day, banked 3 days), "delivering", the Landmark and Wonder stages. The table stays only for trunk prices.
ShownOn the "This week" row, clickable: This week · 🏗 Water Tower 12% · wants sand · Ben can ›. Only active residents are named, and nothing scolds. The bonus shows honestly in g/min on the card.
F5

One return card

M

One card replaces the report and the way-back pages 1–2. It is built by the server on its clock, for every return with news, with one button and keys trapped. The landing screen remains only for the Quiet Isles or crates.

The sections, in order, six lines at most:

  1. gold and level;
  2. your people: resting, a tend that repairs, the stand-in, back, moved in, what your goods did for them, and for a player without a crew, who answered them in the district chat and when;
  3. to look at: stuck buildings and storm damage with Repair all;
  4. the project;
  5. the Invest line (section 5), the lowest, folded first.
F6

Trade with a face, and things a friend needs

M + M · after F1, F2, F4
  • "Where it goes" names both ends (⚡ to the Yard → Dev's Sand Dredger · 8/min · 1.75 g) and the project. The top bar counts paid Yard, Ask and project income, with a hover split.
  • The first gift is announced, to a crew mate and to the project ("∴ Your sand reached the Water Tower: 15 a minute"), within the event budget. A 💛 Thanks tap, once a day per pair. Pairs are shown only to their ends.
  • "Who could use your sand" lists real takers on a producer's panel; F2's button already lays the pipe, so there is one fix button per panel.
  • Interdependence: the advisor also offers factories whose missing input a neighbour has spare ("Cleo has 6 iron ingots spare: a Lab piped to your Yard gate gets them by itself"), and recipes prefer such goods.
  • The toolbar's "Station" becomes "Neighbours". Its first line: "To give a neighbour something, pipe the building that makes it to your Yard gate (G). It goes to whoever here lacks it, and to the station's project."
  • The seat card says what's really there ("wind kit · sand: a dredger on the shore, Level 5"): the Riverside seat promised Ben sand he never had.
F7

The seat picker tells the truth

S · ships early
  • After a refusal the picker reloads with the next seat named ("Tea Time Pipes just settled in Capital NW and keeps its seats for friends. Here's the next free seat: …").
  • It never keeps a stale seat, and Settle is off on a seat that can't be picked.
  • Friends and a stranger are suggested different stations, and "A station of my own" prefers four seats.
  • An honest text when no human has a free seat, and an empty company name says so.
F8

Smart Max

S–M
  • The Max button itself buys only the levels the building's own pipes (and what its gate brings now) keep at ≥ 90%. It shows its price and its reason: Max +8 · 27.5k g, with "+34 would run at 3%: its pipes bring 6 of 204 iron ingots · more…" under it.
  • "More…" opens the choice; the neighbours' spare is a separate line there.
  • Every upgrade button shows its price. Complexes are priced right. There's no confirm on big spends, which we want players to make.
F9

Friends by name, the crew where people look, a tend that does a chore

S–M
  • "Who is this seat for?" makes "Copy Ben's link". The move-in screen reads "Ana kept this seat for Ben", and to the crew he shows as Riverside Co. (Ben).
  • The crew gets its own top-bar spot, out of the clipped name.
  • Tend shows only when it repairs ("💛 Tend: repair Ben's 2 storm-damaged moilies"). There's no fee waiver.
  • Stand-ins copy what partners actually took, not what was matched. A 14-day median comes only if run 2 shows a hole.
F10

Trust in minute one, the phone, small breakers

S–M
  • Rates are read after the solve, so "+0/min" goes. No "Something is in the way" after a placement that worked.
  • On a phone: one toast at a time, the level-up after Loady's first step, a pipe step that says to pick the Pipe tool (and Show me picks it), and a building panel as a bottom sheet of at most 55%.
  • Overlays turn off when their step is done, and Wind and Sun get a legend.
  • Chat gets day stamps and "new since you left". Buttons get distinct accessible names, and research cards are named by their tech.
  • The pipe groups light up on the map (after F2's button).
F11

The edges

S each
  • Link offers wait until 24 h after the other side's next session (at most 14 days) and show on their return card.
  • The lone-human card gives an honest text when no livelier seat is free, and suggests a link only for a real spare–lack pair.
  • A research queue of five.

Dropped: the crew chat channel, the keystone valve, hub research points, a "send to a friend" button, new gold sinks, a 25% spend confirm, and a top-bar project chip. Letting goods pass through buildings is dropped too, unanimously: we explain the rule instead. It comes back only if Ana's replay still fails after F2.

7 · What would change the plan

  • Ana's replay still ends with an idle factory after F2: the rule is the problem. Let a building pass on goods it doesn't use, or lay the pipe for her, before F4 ships.
  • Run 2 pays and shows trade, but nobody knowingly gives by day 8: giving needs an act (an Ask, or "I make it" on the building panel).
  • The project is seen and reachable but under 50% by day 8: the size is wrong.
  • The trade share stays under 15% at the chosen price: seat kits get one consumer of a neighbour's specialty, a chain that pays at that price.
  • Run 2's diaries name project gifts but nobody builds anything for a friend by day 14: the project is carrying the crew alone. Same lever.

What it costs

  • The build: 16 playable commits. The engineer's estimate is 8–10 working days; at my pace it is faster.
  • The agent replays: about 0.3M tokens.
  • The second run: about 1.3M tokens and 70 minutes of agent time, the same as run 1.
