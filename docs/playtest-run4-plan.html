TransportPipes · after AI playtest run 4 · my own reading of the designers' answer

The pipe is the ladder

You asked me to reason about the designers' answer myself, with the whole history in hand. I checked their claims against the code and run 4's numbers, and found one thing they circled but didn't say outright: the pipe tier is the game's real level cap. Upgrades keep paying back within hours at every level: the hub's price falls with what you sell, but stops at a quarter of the base, after which revenue grows in step with output. The only thing that stops a building earning more is the pipe that carries its goods. Everybody in run 4 stayed on Grey Pipes all game, so from about day 7 nothing gold could buy made more money. The way up (Copper, then Steel) was there, but behind research that four of the six AI players never did, and shown only in a stuck building's own panel.

So I keep the designers' plan in its shape, but rebuild it around one ladder that every player can see and climb: Grey → Copper → Steel, each step opening a decade of upgrades that pay. Gold climbs the ladder, friends' copper and steel make it cheaper, and the shared storeys raise everyone's pipes together.

What I found, beyond the designers

1 · The pipe tier is the real level cap

The hub pays full price for each good up to its order (about 1,000 g/min of each good per player in Era 1, × 1.5 each Era), then √(order ÷ sold), but never less than a quarter of the base (HubMarket.cs:54, progression.yaml hubMinMultiplier: 0.25). Past 16× the order, every extra unit sells at a quarter price: revenue grows in step with output again. Levels cost × 1.15 for × 1.10 output, plus a doubling at 10, 25, 50 and 75. Put together, an upgrade pays back within a day at nearly every level. Here is Cleo's Iron Mine, from her real level 21 (210 iron ore a minute), with the game's own formulas:

So the levels are a sink that grows with income, with paybacks a player likes, as long as the pipe carries the output. In run 4 it didn't: the panel said "Level 21: +0 g/min: its pipes carry 205 iron ore a minute" (cleo d06 #220), and that was true. Each pipe tier opens about a decade of output, and with it millions of gold of upgrades that pay. That is the "reason to grow" you asked about, and it already exists in the game, one door away.

The light bots agree: they press the advisor's steps without reading. The seats that made copper climbed and held 2.7–5.1 days of income on day 28; the seats without copper held 10.8–18.9. The other exit is the Trading Post (600 a minute at level 1, more with each level), but that one is gold-only already, and the advisor offers it.

2 · Nobody climbed it

  • All 1,087 pipe tiles stayed grey to day 36, though Cleo made 237k copper ingots.
  • Copper Pipes is a 200-point, 10-minute research (research.yaml), but research needs a Lab with energy and ingots. Only Cleo and Dev researched anything; Ana, Ben, Mia, Mia-B and Jo never did (their only "research" was the station-wide Refinements Cleo and Dev made). Mia's Lab sat idle without energy all month.
  • The next tier was offered only inside a full building's idle status (GameWorld.IdleTexts.cs:150). Cleo researched Copper Pipes on day 6 at 20:44, six hours after she last opened a full building, and never opened one again. The upgrade panel, where every player looks to spend, pointed nowhere, and the advisor went quiet when you already make the ingots (GameWorld.Advisor.cs:548).

3 · Gold isn't what limits growth, so gold has no job

Prices already grow: parcels double (5k up to 10.2M), levels × 1.15, each copy of a building × 1.07. Cleo, at Level 29, could have bought four more parcels for 300k and had two. She had nothing to put on them, because her inputs, pipes and hub orders were all full. Outposts (from Level 15) were never seen by anyone. Making things dearer wouldn't help. Gold needs to buy past the real limits: the pipe, a material nobody near makes, and a shared goal.

4 · The designers were right about the rest

  • The Era wonder's size was a bug. I confirmed the cause: the daily resize counts only players seen in 3 days, and a delivery freezes the size. Both stages were 21.6k of goods instead of about 1.6M.
  • Caps became dead buttons. The order half was used up, the button vanished, and Ana and Ben were stuck.
  • The wish to spend was social. Five offers of gold went into the chat, and all 30,660 g the new sinks took went into the one shared door in sight.
  • After the Landmark the crew had nothing to build together. Days 18–36: fun 2.83, "come back" yes 2 of 6.

The plan, in build order

Each step is a playable commit with tests and an e2e script, as before. Days are for me, building alone.

Milestone A · gold has a job (about 7 days)

1

The fixes run 4 found · 1 day

  • Wonder sizing: count players seen in 7 days, never size from the floor, resize upward only until a quarter has arrived.
  • The Wonders tab says what's ordered and why the rest comes by pipe: "The order half is in: Cleo, Ana · the rest comes by pipe · who can make it ▸".
  • A building that waits says so: it shows "waits for 100 bricks" instead of "is being built".
  • The run-4 small breakers: stale Next steps, the refusal toast left behind, Loady's +0 upgrade step.
2

The pipe ladder · 3 days

Copper Pipes for everyone at Company Level 8, Steel Pipes for everyone when Era 2 begins (the research nodes stay as the Lab's prerequisites). On the upgrade panel of a building at its pipe, one button goes the whole way, with one preview:

Its Grey Pipe is full: 205 iron ore a minute · [ Copper Pipe on its 2 links, then Max to 30 · 34 ingots + 212k · +1,094 g/min ▸ ]
ingots: 12 in your Storage · 22 from Cleo (Yard, 1.3×) or order now (2×)
  • The ingots come from your Storage, then a friend's spare through the Yard, then an order at 2× when nobody near makes them.
  • At Copper's limit: Widen ×2 (1 hour of your income), then ×4, charged per link.
  • The ladder is in sight in the goal tracker, and the advisor's three dead ends become the next rung.

Replaces: "+N would make nothing more", the door only in idle status, the research gate on the first two tiers, grey's silent wall.

3

One order rule, in words · 1½ days

Nobody near makes the good: anyone may order all of it at once at 2× base. Someone near makes it: they get their window first, and half of a shared goal comes by pipe. This one rule covers wants, pipe ingots, Era stages and storeys. The button never vanishes. A waiting building names the chain that would make its good, offered first to the friend with gold and no maker role.

4

Your gold can · 2 days

The top bar reads "40.6M · 24 days ▸". Tapping it opens three lines:

  1. the open shared goal, with Give;
  2. a want nobody near can fill;
  3. "Grow everything that pays": the next rung on every full link, then Max where payback is within 7 days, in one preview, one tap, with Undo.

The same top line leads the return card and never folds. Every door counts what was shown and what was pressed.

✓

Check A: replays from run 4's snapshots · ½ day + about 0.3M tokens

Three single sessions on the new build, from the snapshots before them: Cleo day 6 (her long session, first full pipes), Ana day 27 (the Cathedral and the Kiln), Ben day 36 (back after a month, on his phone). Plus the light bots and the pacing run. Pass:

  • each of the three replays spends at least 20% of the gold it opens with, by its own taps;
  • every bot holds 5 days of income or less on day 28;
  • Level 30 comes on day 25 ± 2.
If it fails: the doors still aren't enough, and we look again before building more.

Milestone B · something to build together (about 5 days + art)

5

The Landmark grows storeys · 3½ days + 1–2 days of art

When a station's Landmark stands, its first storey opens; the next opens the day after one stands. A storey has:

  • a goods part: 6 hours of the station's own spare, in goods a playing resident makes;
  • a purse: 1 day × 1.2ⁿ⁻¹ of the station's 7-day income, fixed when the storey opens. Givers give 1 h, 6 h or 1 day of their own income, as often as they like, with no cap.

Each storey gives +10% on its residents' pipes (up to +50%) and a plaque naming the givers in hours of their own income. On run 4's numbers, Capital SE's first four storeys would cost about 6.1M, 7.3M, 8.8M and 10.5M. Storey art goes through process.mjs and fit.mjs.

6

The player alone is heard · 1 day

If nobody else in her station has chatted, her chat opens on the district channel; a newcomer's hello shows as a line in crews' chats; every gift that stands a stage is named at both ends, strangers included.

✓

Check B, then run 5 · the bots again, a replay of day 21, then the full story

Run 5 plays the same story again on a fresh world (about 1.6M tokens for the players), so its numbers line up with runs 1–4.

In all: about 12 days of building and 1–2 of art, plus the checks. The designers' estimate was 11½–12½ plus art: the ladder and the counting add a little, and dropping grey Widen and the per-giver cap takes a little away.

What I need from you

  1. Copper and Steel Pipes without research (Copper at Company Level 8, Steel with Era 2). This is my strongest change to the designers' plan; it touches the tech tree as designed in the GDD. Yes or no?
  2. Two milestones with replays in between, or build all six steps and go straight to run 5? I recommend the milestones: a cheap answer in about a week, before the storeys and their art.
