gone differently, and how we would see it. One correction to my own round 1 first. The Lab's "15 research at 3%" is
not a bug: it is 3% of 509 points a minute (economy 5, engineer E12). Cleo's pile of points comes from running one

- **Engineer (replay E1–E3 from row07; Mia from 64% to about 80% of Dev).** Use row10 first: the Lab is already at
  level 35 there and Mia's dead House is in place, so the replay needs no actions. Advance 6 h. Pass: Ben ≥ 70 g/min
  (run 1: 0.0), Ana ≥ 200 (77), Dev ≥ 120 (44–57), and Mia's real growth up from 70 to ≥ 90, each equal to hub rate +
  paid Yard within 2%. Your 80% then follows from her rate. Row07 stays for Cleo's Max (forced): no neighbour moves

- **Economy (five metrics).** Yes to all: paid Yard gold beside matched, the share of the bank spent per session, the
  stage by day, the share traded t, and income per command (and per building). The bank share and income per command
  are diagnostics, not pass marks: a game that spent the bank for you would pass them without being more fun.
- **Clarity (stuck-to-running play minutes; when each player first sees the Next steps; are row08 and row10 right?).**
  Play minutes from stuck to running become my main dead-end measure (pass: ≤ 10 owner play minutes, no STUCK). Row08
  is right for Ana (factory stuck since day 4, her d08 session) and Mia's stale step. Row10 is right for Dev's d14 and
  Ana's d14. Add Dev's d10 (row08 has no Dev yet: script his d08 move-in from `actions.jsonl`, then advance to day 10), because
  he builds the dredger in that session: the test is
  whether the panel catches it the same session. The first session with the Next steps is a fine diagnostic. On the
  phone, also count the cards and toasts on Ben's first screen.
- **Together (which measure; is Thanks the right size?).** "First good knowingly given" needs two halves. The game half:
  a paid flow between two crew mates (or into the project), whose first-flow event was shown on both first screens
  (logged). The player half: the giver names it in a diary or chat. A log can't tell that someone *saw* it, and a diary
  alone can't tell it was paid. Thanks: the right size for Ben (*"not letting Ana down"*, guilt in 3 of 5 diaries), too
  small for Cleo (*"no payoff for helping"*). Cleo's reward is the price, shown. Keep Thanks (S), but expect little use:
  the receiver must open a panel on the "from" side, and Ana only ever opened her stuck factory.

## What everyone missed

- **The friends had nothing to trade.** Every starter kit is energy, and Loady sends everyone to iron ore. The engineer
  warns that with true lacks the Yard will trade *less*. In run 1's world it would trade almost nothing between friends.
  So "playing together" in week 1 rests on the project and on one receiver card (Ana's Lab for Cleo's ingots), not on
  the Yard. Run 2 should watch for exactly that pair.
- **The seat promised Ben sand.** The picker said *"Riverside: water and sand, wood"*. His House left 3 Wind Moilys,
  and his land had no sand. That promise drove 36 days of guilt. The seat card should say what is really there (*"wind
  kit · sand: a dredger on the shore, Level 5"*), or the kit should keep the promise. Only the analyst raised it.
- **Order matters, and no paper gives the order across papers.** Make it true, then show it. The Yard (E1, E2) before
  paid income on screens. The dead-end fix before the Next steps go to everyone. The recipe and size before any
  project chip or card.
- **The snapshots hold old baskets.** A new recipe won't show in a row07 or row10 replay unless a dev cheat
  (`project restage`) starts the current stage again under the new rules. Database changes during the fixes must stay
  additive EF migrations, or the replays die.
- **New phone hurdles.** Together 4 asks kit holders to pipe to the gate themselves. Ben's only real STUCK was a pipe
  drag (d03 #60). Every new pipe step needs the planner button, and a check on a phone (`touch.mjs`).
- **The +3% won't sell the project to Cleo.** It is about +24 g/min (her own sum, d21 #303), less than the Quarry the
  next steps offered her (+30 for 3,000 g). The project's pull is the crew and the building on the map (Ana's one
  "together 4"), not its bonus. Show the bonus honestly, but don't rely on it.
