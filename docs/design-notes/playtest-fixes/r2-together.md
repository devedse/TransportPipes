# Round 2 · together

I read all five papers. My test for every proposal: would Ana, Ben, Cleo and Dev then play together *on purpose*
(the §17 promises; principles 1, 5, 6 and 7)? And does it keep Mia and the sleepers whole?

## Answers to the questions put to me

**Engineer: with true lacks the Yard trades less. Can it still carry "playing together"?**
- **Not alone, and not in week 1.** With E2, almost every flow of this run disappears. Every starter kit makes energy,
  and Loady sends everyone to an iron mine. What is left is tiny: Cleo's factory 0.7 iron ore short (cleo d21-1900
  #300), Ana's ore.
- **Asks and links can't carry it either.** An Ask fills the asker's storages, which no building of Ana's needed.
  Links serve companies *outside* the station: Ana looked there for Cleo (ana d27-2000 #259).
- **What carries it in week 1 is the project.** With economy 2c, it buys what every resident already makes: energy and
  iron ore. So all four give from the first evening, and the game says so. The keystone is the one decision made on
  purpose.
- **What carries it from week 2 is chains across two estates.** On day 21, Cleo had 6.1 iron ingots spare. Ana wanted
  a Lab and believed she had no way to get ingots (ana d21-1900 #221, #241). A Lab piped to Ana's gate would have got
  them at 1.75 g by itself. Nobody's paper tells her so (see "What everyone missed"). The honest Yard is the engine;
  the project and these chain suggestions give it a reason to run.

**Economy: is 72 hours right for the keystone valve?**
- **No.** These players came every 2–7 days. The Water Tower card was first opened on day 21 (cleo #302) and day 27
  (ana #261).
- A 72-hour valve lets the richest player buy away the crew's one decision before half the crew has seen it. Cleo had
  1.6M gold on day 6, and the keystone's rest would cost about 16k.
- **Amend:**
  - the valve opens 7 days after the stage starts;
  - only once every active resident has had the keystone line on their return card;
  - no credit, grey on the ring (as you propose).
- I want a valve, because of principle 3. Ben, the natural sand and stone maker, stopped on day 3. Without a valve, a
  stage could wait for a sleeper.

**Clarity: what goes in the return card's station section, and on the crew's top-bar line?** At most three station
lines, in this order:

1. **A friend's arrival or absence.** At most one, the newest:
   - *"🎈 Dev moved in next door (Windy Heights)"*
   - *"💤 Riverside Co. (Ben) is resting, 4 days: his estate runs and earns in full."* `[💛 Tend]`
   - *"⚙ Ben hasn't played for 3 weeks: from day 33 Granny Ida ⚙ keeps your trades with him going. His seat is kept to
     day 93."*
   - *"🎈 Ben is back."*
2. **What you gave:** *"🤝 Your energy built 18% of the Water Tower and ran Cleo's factory: +4.1k g."* Only when it
   happened.
3. **The project, as an invitation:** *"🏗 The Water Tower is at 34%. It wants stone, and you have stone on your land
   (Quarry · Level 10)."*

I object to your draft line *"nobody here has sent any yet"*. It scolds (§20: "a return that starts with … a
scolding").

The crew's top-bar line shows one item, by priority:
1. a tend that is possible (*"Ben resting · 💛 tend"*);
2. a keep-seat card;
3. a friend's Ask;
4. *"Ben is back"*.

**"For: Ben"** on holds is exactly my naming. Take your format, "Riverside Co. (Ben)", everywhere crew mates appear.

The Yard's one-line answer to "How do I send sand to Ana?" will be true once E1, E2 and economy 2 land:
*"Pipe your sand to your Yard gate (G): the Water Tower and any neighbour whose buildings use sand take it by
themselves."*

**Researcher: what gives the crew complementary needs on evening one? Can the game say "your sand reached the Water
Tower"?**
- **On evening one, nothing honest but the project.** I'd not fake needs or give kits by seat: the kit's energy sells
  at once, and that is its job.
- **Days 2–5: the seats' home goods.** Design §14 puts each seat's home goods in its Level-5 and Level-7 parcels. Let the
  keystone come from a resident's buyable land, not only bought land (an amendment to economy 2c and E8b). The Water
  Tower's sand then points at Ben: Riverside, a shore in his Level-5 parcel. That is the crew's decision: Ben buys his
  shore, or Dev does on his own land.
- **Yes, the game should say it.** One event per giver and good, the first time it lands: *"∴ Your sand reached the
  Water Tower: 15 a minute."* After that, a line on the return card (item 2 above). This is your "first good knowingly
  given", visible in the game itself.

## Where I object

1. **The keystone valve at 72 h (economy 2f).** See above. Principle 1 needs something to decide together, and that
   needs at least one session of every friend. Amend to 7 days and "seen by all active residents".
2. **My own crew channel (my 8). I withdraw it; the researcher is right.** The friends talked fine outside the game (37
   messages). Worse for Mia: what made her world feel alive was the crew writing in the *district* chat. Cleo answered
   her on day 2, Ana on day 4, Dev on day 21 (mia d08-2000 #104, d15-2100 diary), and Ana wrote to Cleo in Capital
   chat on day 36 (ana #278). A crew channel would have taken that talk away from the one stranger who read it. The
   crew's news goes on the return card and the crew line.
3. **"Mia never got neighbours was mostly the story" (researcher, differs 4).** In part. On the testers' server (small
   mode), a crew filling the only lit station is the normal case, not a quirk of the story. A stranger landing next
   door to a closed crew station must still have a way to people. Two code facts:
   - the lone-human card can't appear when no livelier station has a free seat (Return.cs:208, 247);
   - a link offer lapses after 72 h (Links.cs:96), while Mia played 6–7 days apart.
   Jo tests placement. These two need fixing whatever the placement.
4. **Max counting the Yard's spare (economy 3: "got + network spare + Yard spare").** I side with clarity: count only
   the building's own pipes for "runs at full speed". Then mention the neighbours separately: *"With your neighbours'
   spare energy (about 300 a minute at 1.75 g) it could run at 40%."* Otherwise Max levels lean on a friend's spare
   that can move away. With "usual buyers first" it won't vanish overnight, but the friend never chose it.
5. **The Invest step (economy 3).** One guard: when it levels a *consumer*, count only the player's own supply, as
   for Max. Otherwise it builds the next Lab trap: paid after E2, but a surprise for the friend whose spare it takes.

## What everyone missed

1. **The receiver's bridge: a neighbour's spare as an input.** No paper makes the advisor's chain steps count what
   neighbours have spare. *"Cleo has 6 iron ingots a minute spare: a Lab piped to your Yard gate gets them at 1.75 g,
   by itself."* This is principle 5 at its purest: one chain across two estates, through the Yard. It is the only
   honest answer to Ana's *"Does the Yard hand them to me automatically if I just ask?"* (group, day 27).
2. **The stand-in copies one moment of *matched* flows.** `NewStandIn` (StandIns.cs:47–56) takes the sleeper's gate
   flows at the minute of sleep, using `Delivered`: what was sent, not what partners took. The design says "the
   sleeper's 14-day median" (§9).
   - Today a false match at that minute becomes 14 days of a stand-in selling into nothing.
   - A quiet minute leaves a friend's real supply uncovered.
   - The fix: keep a 14-day rolling of *paid* flows per partner and good (it needs E4's figure). S–M.
   Principle 3: players who stop leave no holes. Today the hole depends on luck.
3. **Every "next step" proposal depends on clarity 2.1.** Engineer E5's dead-end step, economy's Invest and my
   together cards all sit in a panel that 4 of 5 players never saw. Ship 2.1 with the first of them, or none of them
   counts.
4. **The giver needs to hear that it landed.** Only the researcher asked. The first-gift event, the return line and
   the Thanks tap (my 2) are the "payoff for helping" Cleo missed (cleo d10-2000 diary). They cost no gold.
5. **Explicit pairs need a privacy guard.** Once the Yard names who buys from whom, show a pair only to its two ends.
   The design says other members' flows "are not listed, so the panel is never a list of who didn't deliver" (§7).
   Nobody wrote it down for E4 or my 2.
6. **Link offers lapse before a weekly player sees them** (Links.cs:96, 551). For a lonely player a link is the only
   bridge to people. An offer should wait until 24 h after the other side's next session, at most 14 days, and show on
   their return card.
