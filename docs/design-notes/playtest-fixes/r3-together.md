play together on purpose (the §17 promises; principles 1, 5, 6 and 7), and do Mia and the sleepers stay whole?

## VOTE A

**As proposed: the line on the return card, Show me opening the best upgrade, no one-tap spend.** An unseen big spend
is exactly what hurt Cleo, and her friends with her. But economy is right that hour-payback upgrades were never shown
(21 of 23 sessions spent under 3% of the bank). On the card the line comes after the people and the project.

## My last dissent

**In week 1 the project is the only honest gift. The plan must let players hear it, and must count it.**

1. **The evidence.**
   - Every kit is energy. Every estate has its own iron, stone and forest (WorldGenerator.cs 287–351).
   - With F1's true lacks, human-to-human trade in this run would have been about zero (r2-economy, missed 1;
     r2-researcher, missed 1).
   - So from evening one to about day 10, the friends give each other nothing in the Yard. What they give, they give
     to the Foundation and the Water Tower.
2. **What the plan says.**
   - F6's first-gift event is "for human pairs only".
   - Run 2's main measure reads "a paid flow seen on both first screens + named by the giver". A project has no second
     screen.
3. **What follows.** Ana's energy builds the Foundation in minutes, and nobody is told they gave. Run 2 then fails its
   first pass mark ("one pair by day 2 20:00") by construction: the week we fix would look like the week we failed.
4. **The fix (S).**
   - The first-gift event and the return card's "what your goods did" include project deliveries.
   - The pass mark reads: *"a paid flow to a crew mate or the station's project, shown to the giver, and named by the
     giver in diary or chat"*.
   - The human-pair half stays as a second mark: every crew member by day 14, as the plan has it.
5. **What would change my mind.** If run 2's diaries name project gifts but nobody ever builds anything *for a friend*
   by day 14, then the project is carrying the crew alone. That is the plan's own trigger: t < 15% → kits with one
   consumer of a neighbour's specialty.

### Still at risk after the plan, and what run 2 must watch

- **t, the share traded between humans.**
  - Watch: by day 10, does any crew member build a consumer for a friend's spare (the receiver card, the neighbour-fed
    chain step)?
  - If nobody has by day 14, the friends only ever met in the project. Pull the kits lever forward.
- **The keystone as a decision.**
  - Watch: is it named in the group chat by day 3, and who builds it?
  - If Ben stops before he can, does the re-pick (F4b) hand it to an active friend without a gap?
- **Tending (rows 6, 20).** Count the Tend buttons shown against the taps. A tend shown and never tapped is fine. A
  tend never shown means row 6 promised a ritual the game has no need for: then rewrite the promise, don't invent a
  chore.
- **The six-line card.** On days with storms, a stuck building and the Invest line, does "your people" survive? Log
  which lines were folded into "and N more".
- **Mia without Jo.** Run 2 seats Jo, so it can't show whether a stranger alone next to a closed crew station is told
  the truth. One scripted check (`seat-race.mjs` with no human seat free) must cover it.
- **The phone on evening one.** Ben's first screen must show one toast at a time (the first-gift events count
  against the budget).
