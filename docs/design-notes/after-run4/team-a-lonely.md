# Round A · lonely

I am the guardian of players alone and players who stop. My two players in run 4:
- **Mia**, beside Jo, a neighbour who never chats, with a session every 7 days;
- **Ben**, back on day 36 with a month's income and 5 minutes on a phone.

Both failed my marks:
- **Mia** went from 9 to 10 buildings between days 8 and 15 and spent 0.39%. Her "alive" was 2, 2, 2, 3, lower than
  run 3 (findings, marks "The player alone" and "Mia: alive").
- **Ben** spent 11.6k of 13.5M (0.09%). He wrote: *"the thing my friends need from me is hidden"* and *"13.4M gold
  and nothing I understand to spend it on"* (ben d36).

## 1 · Diagnosis: income arrives in bulk, spending comes in crumbs

The nine steps were the right doors, built at the wrong size, in the wrong place, and with a cap where it mattered.
From my seat one cause sits under the analyst's four.

**1. An idle game pays by the week, and our doors charge by the click.**

Every session of a rare player brings days of income at once:

| Player | Away | Brought back | Spent that session | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mia | 6 days | 2.2M | about 30k (34.6k spent by day 14) | mia d08 #87; marks |
| Mia | 7 days | 7.6M | about 37k | mia d15 #119 |
| Ben | 33 days | 13M | 3.4k, one Max on a moily | ben d36 #77–#82 |

- The things to buy cost 3–60k each, one building at a time (findings, `gold`).
- A 5–15 minute session can't spend a week's income at 10k a click, and many clicks preview "+0" at the pipe.
- So the bank grows by about a day of income every day, exactly the run's chart. **The rarer the player, the worse the
  mismatch:** Ben's month met a two-tap session.

**2. The bots show it.**
- The advisor's light bots banked 2.7–18.9 days on day 28, against the players' 23.6–29.1
  (`light-bots-day28.md`).
- They walked the same doors step after step: Cleo's bot has an Iron Mine at level 69 and nine Small Factories at 50.
- People took one or two steps and distrusted the rest: *"'would make nothing more' again - does it even earn? dont
  trust"* (ben d36 #82).
- **The doors are long chains of small steps, and people don't walk chains.** Idle games spend with "buy max" and
  "upgrade all", sized to the pile.

**3. The big doors were capped exactly where my players live.**
- An order may fill at most half of a Wonder stage, and the other half needed bricks, steel and concrete nobody in the
  Capital made: the Dynamo stopped at 89%, the Cathedral at 50%.
- For a player alone, or a station of strangers, "nobody near makes it" is the normal case, not the edge.
- And both stages were sized at the floor (6 h × 60 g/min ≈ 21.6k g of goods), so even fully open they were crumbs.

**4. What my players looked for was hidden.**
- Ben went to Hub › Wonders for the Cathedral and found "only bars" and no button (ben d36 #88–#94).
- The friend's-want line we promised returners (our run-3 rule) had nothing to show: the only two wants were Dev's
  and Ana's brick scaffolds, which their owners read as "is being built".

**5. Being alone got worse while the gold piled up.** The analyst's "a stranger whose gifts and words don't reach the
crew" is my seat's finding of the run:
- Mia's chat opened on a station channel holding only Jo, who never chats; her five greetings went unanswered until
  Mia-B found her in the district chat on day 15 (director's observations, rows 1, 5 and 11).
- Mia-B's wood finished the crew's Water Tower, and nobody, her included, was told.
- Gold can't buy company, but a branch next to people can.

**Our own triggers have fired.** We deferred the Landmark tiers and the branch "only if run 4 or the bots show gold
still piling up … also for a player alone if her mark fails" (`docs/playtest-run3-design.html`). The bots bank up to
18.9 days, the Landmark stood on day 17, and Mia's mark failed. **By our own rule they are due.**

## 2 · Proposal: "Spend it in one tap, on something people see"

Five parts. A is the cause from my seat; B and C are the analyst's recommendations 1–2, sized; D is our deferred door;
E is recommendations 4–5 for the player alone.

### A · One big buy, sized to your bank, where you return

On every return card with at least a day of income banked, the first growth line (under the people line) offers **one
plan sized to the bank**, built from what the advisor already knows to pay:

> 🏗 **Grow everything that pays · 4.1M of your 13.4M · +420 g/min · back in 7 days ▸**
> *every producer to its pipe's limit · copper pipes on 3 full links (ingots ordered) · Show me what changes*

**What it is:**
- The advisor's steps that pay, bought together: Max on every building whose preview pays, and the pipe tier on every
  link at its limit, with its ingots filled by your own Smelter or ordered.
- One preview (total price, total g/min, payback), then one tap.
- **Show me** draws what changes: pipes turning copper, buildings rising a level.

One tap, Undo for an hour as for any buy; never automatic, never while away.

**Where else it shows:**
- the top bar's gold, as a button: *"Spend: 3 plans worth it ▸"*;
- the Next steps (ranked by the bank, as F3's effort term already does).

It replaces the card's single "Max pays: upgrade a moily" (ben d36 #77) with the genre's "buy max" for a whole
estate: chosen, previewed, undoable, and the only spending that fits a 5-minute return.

### B · When nobody near makes it, gold finishes it (recommendation 1, adopted)

**The rule.** For a want or a Wonder stage whose good no lit resident of the big station (or, for the wonder, of the
Capital) makes:
- the friends' window is zero (our rule);
- **the order may fill all of it, not half**, at 2.0 × base, over 6 hours;
- it is credited 25%, with no 💛.

**The button never vanishes.** It says what it is:

> *"Half ordered (Cleo 9,000 g) · nobody in the Capital makes bricks · Order the other half · 4,500 g ▸ · or who could
> make them ▸"*

**What's left for friends:** the goods they make count in full and are named, and the chain pointer (recommendation 3)
offers the role. Once a lit maker exists, the window returns.

**The returner's line** our run-3 design promised now always has something to show:

> *"The Copper Cathedral waits for 125 steel · nobody here makes steel · Order it for the crew ▸ 15k"*

### C · Shared goals sized in days, open to gold, for everyone near

**The Era wonder:** each stage is **1 day of the Capital's income**, measured over 7 days. On run 4's day 21 that is
about 9M. Half comes by pipe, gold the rest (all of it under B).

**The station's Landmark tiers (now):** after the Landmark, the station builds **public works** tier after tier:
- **Gold:** 1, 2, 4, 8… days of the station's 7-day income, from any resident with one tap (*"Give a day of your
  income ▸"*).
- **Materials:** one tier-2 good the station makes, by pipe, orderable under B.
- **What each tier gives:**
  - a visible addition to the Landmark (new art);
  - +1% production for every seat, at most +10%;
  - the givers' names on its plaque, never who didn't.
- **Who takes part:** the stranger next door and a small station's residents are residents.

**The scale, from run 4's numbers:**
- **Capital SE** (Ana, Ben, Cleo, Dev, Mia-B next door) earned about 5,300 g/min on day 21: 7.6M a day. Tiers 1–3 are
  53M, against the crew's 100M banked then.
- **Mia's Capital NW** (Mia 980, Jo 199 g/min): 1.7M a day. Tiers 1–3 are 11.9M, against her 17.2M.
- **These are sinks the size of the banks.** The nine steps' were 21.6k.

### D · The branch next to people (our deferred door, now due)

**The rule:**
- From Level 15, a second estate (the Franchise exists) for **2 days of the buyer's income**.
- Placed by "Find me neighbours": a crew mate's station first, and **for a player alone, next to a crew that plays**.
- It trades like any neighbour. No free transfer between your estates.

**Why:** new land is the one thing a full estate can't make, expansion with no reset; and for Mia it buys people
next door with her own pile.

### E · The player alone is heard (recommendations 4 and 5, adopted)

**The chat:**
- If no other human at her station has chatted, her chat opens on the district channel, and her hello goes there.
- A newcomer's hello also shows as one line in crews' chats.
- Run 3's Mias greeted the district and answered each other on day 1; run 4's met on day 15 (director's
  observations, row 1).

**The gifts:** every gift that stands a stage is named at both ends, strangers and resting friends included: *"mia_pipes
Pipes' wood finished your Water Tower 💛"*; *"Your wood finished The Pipe Dreamers' Water Tower 💛"*.

## 3 · The five questions

**1. Can gold finish what no friend near makes?** **Yes, at once after a zero window, all of it, at 2.0 × base over 6
hours, credited 25%.**
- Where a lit maker exists, the window and the friends-first fill stay as built.
- What's left for friends: everything they make, named and fully credited, and the chain pointer (B).

**2. How every late cost is sized:**

| Cost | Sized in | Measured |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wonder stages | 1 day of the Capital's income | 7-day average of production income of players seen in the last 14 days |
| Landmark tiers | 1, 2, 4, 8 days of the station's income | the same |
| Branch | 2 days of the buyer's own income | the same |
| The plan (A) | up to the bank | — |
| Orders | 2.0 × base | — |

**The sizing bug's fix:**
- Never size while no player is lit: wait until one is.
- Size from the 7-day average, not the 3-day window.
- Resize upward daily until the first player delivery.
- Never below 6 h of the measured income of whoever is lit.

**3. Where the doors are, when gold is plentiful and the building is at its pipe:**

| Where | What it says |
| --- | --- |
| Return card, first growth line | The plan sized to the bank (A) |
| Top bar | Gold as a button: *"Spend: 3 plans ▸"* |
| Upgrade panel at the pipe | *"Past level 24 this adds nothing: its pipe is full · Copper Pipe for this link · 28 ingots (yours, or ordered for 1,000 g) ▸"*, never a bare "+41 would make nothing more" (recommendation 8) |
| Next steps | Ranked by the bank, with the plan, the Landmark's tier and the branch |
| A friend's or the Capital's waiting good | Its order line (B) on the card and the Wonders tab |

**4. After the Landmark:** the Landmark tiers (C), for as long as the chapter runs. They double, so a station meets a
want its own size every week. They are expansion, never rebuilding.

**5. The bots' mark:** bots walk every advisor step, every session, without fear; people take one or two and stop at
the first "+0". Our spending is a chain of crumbs while income comes in bulk, so spending must come in bulk too (A).
Measure the ≤ 3 days on people, not bots.

## 4 · Cost and risks

| Part | Effort |
| --- | --- |
| A · The plan: Max on all that pays, plus pipe-tier wants, one preview, one tap, Undo | 2–3 days (Max, the what-if and pipe wants exist) |
| B · Orders past half where no maker; the button that explains itself | 1–1.5 days |
| C · Wonder sizing (bug fix plus 1 day of income); Landmark tiers | 1 + 3 days, plus art |
| D · The branch next to people | 1.5 days (the Franchise exists) |
| E · Chat where people are; gifts named at both ends | 1.5 days |
| **Total** | **About 10–12 days** |

**Risks:**
- **A feels like autopilot.** It is chosen, previewed and undoable, and never runs by itself. If diaries say "the
  game spends for me", show its parts as ticks.
- **Spending raises income, so banks grow faster.** Upgrades are growth, which is the owner's ask. C and D don't pay
  back in gold, so they hold the banks.
- **A whale finishes the Era alone.** It is credited 25%, and the Era turns earlier for everyone.
- **Friends matter less.** Only where nobody near makes the good. Where a maker exists, nothing changes.

## 6 · One mark for run 5 that would prove me wrong

his phone).**

If he doesn't, with a plan sized to his bank on his first card, then the cause isn't the size and place of the doors,
and my diagnosis is wrong.
