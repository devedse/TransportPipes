# Round B · economy

I read all six round-A papers. I draft the shared text next, so I start with where we already agree.

## Where the six of us already agree

1. **The diagnosis.** The sinks exist; the levels at 1.15× a level are unlimited. Gold can't reach what binds after
   week 1: the pipe (100 a minute per connection), materials only from your own Storage (`Pay()`), and dealt land
   putting those materials on a friend's seat. The loop "earn → spend on what raises your rate → earn more" breaks
   there. All six cite Cleo's pit (882 made, 100 sold) and the 95–100% unspent banks.
2. **Row 7 is the door.** Every want buys its materials through the existing Ask engine:
   - goods go into the build, never a Storage;
   - friends see the need once and can Send from their Storage, named at both ends, with Thanks.

   It replaces Deliver, "N in your storages", the Plan board's Ask form and the Wonders minigame.
3. **A pipe tier is a want too.** A capped link names its limit and its key in place, and Max stops at the pipe.
4. **The Era wonder is the Capital's want,** sized at each stage's start from the players' measured income. Every card
   tells how it ended.
5. **Row 10:**
   - Labs rest when nothing can be researched.
   - Refinements cost 5k × 1.6ⁿ points, capped per line.
   - Hub orders and the Invest line are priced in hours of income.
6. **Hub orders become buyers at the trading post:** no Storage, 30 minutes of measured income. All six say yes.
7. **Gold may help a friend's want,** paid to the hub or a House, never between players, never between same-device
   accounts. Five of six (not the outsider). Gold-bought goods never carry a 💛 and count 25% toward shared credit.
8. **Marks:**
   - ≥ 20% of earnings spent;
   - crew income +30% and 4+ buildings each from day 14 to day 36;
   - ≥ ⅓ of want materials from friends;
   - the Dynamo before its deadline with 3+ contributors;
   - no "gold feels empty" diary after day 10.

## What I take from the others, and what I drop

**I take:**
- **The paced Order (friends):** the hub at 2.0 × base, shipped evenly over 6 hours. Each round, friends' goods and the
  Yard go first and are taken off the order, and the unused gold is refunded. Anyone in the big station may order for
  anyone's want.
  - It does what my window tried to do, without waiting on anyone. A friend's Brickworks at 12–55 bricks a minute
    beats an order's 0.3–5 a minute on every real want (a Big Factory's 100, the Dynamo's 1,800), even while the friend
    sleeps, because the Yard round runs anyway (`MatchAsks`).
- **"What stops you"** (outsider): one growth line naming the solver's binding limit, its key and its price. Plus
  "wants" as the only word, and a scaffold on the map.
- **Refinements that raise the walls first** (outsider: pipes +10%, trading +10%), **applied to every resident of the
  station and named** (friends: *"Bricks +6% · Cleo's research 💛"*).
- **A true preview and an hour's Undo** (psychologist). Cleo's Lab Max cost her 783 → 285 g/min (cleo d06 #180); Dev
  called a 329k upgrade *"a trap"* (dev d10). Hoarding was partly learned. My one guard: an Undo also takes back the
  XP the build gave.
- **An income-scaled shared want: the station's Landmark tiers** (psychologist). Tier n costs 2ⁿ⁻¹ days of the
  station's summed measured income in gold, plus materials. Gold never finishes a tier, and the names go in the ring.
  It is the same shape as wild's Capital lots and my Commission, at the scale where friends live.
- **"While I'm away"** (lonely): an opt-in budget that orders the crew's waiting wants after 24 h. It gives the absent
  friend a way to matter, and Ben's 5.5M a job.

**I drop:**
- **My 24–72 h window.** It makes a present player wait on an absent one. Lonely's guard is right: a deadline counts
  only lit players. The paced Order keeps the friends' edge without the wait.
- **My Commission as a separate verb.** It becomes the Order on a shared want, with the same cap: gold fills at most
  half of any stage, wonder stage or Landmark tier.

## What I reject, and why

1. **Lonely's "Buy now" at 2.5 × base, at once.** Price can't keep a friend's goods worth anything when banks hold
   28 days of income: 100 bricks at 2.5× is 1,250 g, 0.003% of Ana's 39.8M.
   - The one gift both friends felt in run 3 was goods arriving over days (Ana's stone in Cleo's Brickworks, cleo d14
     #341–#368). An instant buy would have replaced it.
   - Six hours costs Mia nothing: she played on days 1, 2, 8 and 15, so every order lands between her sessions.
2. **The outsider's "gold never buys a want's materials".** It fails the skimmer and the player who stops:
   - Ben played three days on a phone and never built a chain. Under "never", a want of his waits forever, or a
     friend's goods are the only door, which puts the guilt of run 1 back on him.
   - "Cancel, your gold back" is a fine exit, and I keep it after 3 play days unfilled. But an exit is not a door.
   - The outsider's sharpest point is that a 12-hour fill always lands before an async player is back. The paced
     Order's friends-first rule answers it: the friend's running chain fills the want in that time, whether either
     of them is online.
3. **Wild's Widen, gold buying pipe capacity ×2 or ×4 at once.**
   - A ×4 grey link carries 400 a minute, 80% of a Copper Pipe's 500. That removes the reason for the copper ingots,
     which need two seats (copper + coal).
   - The pipe wall is the one place where dealt land made friends needed in weeks 2–3 (outsider, friends,
     psychologist).
   - If gold should buy capacity, let it be shared: a Landmark tier whose perk is "the station's pipes carry +25%",
     funded together. Not a private bypass.
4. **Separate district lots** (wild) **beside the station's Landmark.** One endless shared want is enough for run 4.
   - Both need art for every tier, which is the costly part.
   - The station is where friends live, and where Mia's own small station also grows.
   - Wild's bottleneck perks, such as pipes +25%, go into the Landmark's tiers.

## The three open questions, final

**1. Can gold buy a want's materials? Yes, after a pace, never at once.**
- An Order buys the rest from the hub at **2.0 × base**, shipped evenly over **6 hours**.
- Each round, your own producers, friends' Sends and the station's spare go first and are taken off the order. Unused
  gold comes back.
- Ordered goods go only into that want. They count 25% toward shared credit and never carry a 💛.
- Anyone in the big station may order for anyone's want there. Same-device accounts can't.
- On a shared want (a stage, a wonder stage, a Landmark tier) orders fill at most half.

**2. Do hub orders become buyers? Yes, on the same engine, after the wants' core:**
- a buyer at your trading post for 24 h;
- sized at 30 minutes of measured income;
- paying 2.0 × base;
- personal: nobody Sends to your daily order.

**3. A two-friend keystone? Now, with lonely's guard.**
- It applies from the Water Tower on, only where two **lit** residents (played in the last 3 days at the stage's
  start) each make one land input: bricks (stone + coal) or copper ingots (copper + coal).
- Elsewhere today's rule. The built pass-over still moves it on after 2 days.
- Four of six say now. The guard answers lonely's "presence" worry.
- The outsider is right that the wants already create these chains. The keystone only points the station's own goal
  the same way.
