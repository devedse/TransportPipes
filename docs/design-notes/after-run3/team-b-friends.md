# Round B · friends

All six of us read run 3 the same way:
- gold piles up because the walls stop growth (the pipe at 100 a minute, materials that come only from your own
  Storage);
- the doors in those walls are friends' goods, which nothing could carry.

We also all adopt row 7. Where we really differ is one question: **how long a friend gets before gold finishes a want.**
I settle that first, and the rest follows from it.

## 1 · What I take, and what I drop of my own

**I drop my 6-hour paced Order.**
- The outsider is right that any wait shorter than a friend's absence hands every want to gold. Sessions came 2–7 days
  apart. *"A 12-hour House fill always lands before they're back."*
- Pacing over 6 hours adds a rule without protecting anyone.

**I take a window measured in friends' sessions, not in hours**, from the economy designer: *"until every crew mate who
can make the good has had one session, at most 72 hours."*
- It is the same principle the social analyst gave the keystone clock: it starts once every active resident has seen
  the want.
- During the window, the Yard's spare and Sends fill the want by themselves, as Ana's stone fed Cleo's Brickworks while
  both were away (cleo d14 #366).

**I take lonely's answer for players who have no friend to wait for.** "Never at once" means never for Mia and for a
returner (lonely §3). So:
- **The window is zero when no other player in the big station can make or holds the good.**
- After the window, the hub sells the rest into the build at once, at its own sell price, **2.5 × base**. That is no new
  price (lonely, economy).

**From the psychologist:**
- **A true preview on every purchase,** from the solver: *"+0 g/min: this pipe carries 100"*, *"−498 g/min: the Lab eats
  your ingots"*.
- **An hour's Undo.** Cleo learned to hoard after the Lab's Max cut her income from 783 to 285 (cleo d06 #180).
- **Landmark tiers** as the station's want that grows with income, replacing my "station levels". Her numbers are
  better.

**From the outsider:**
- **"What stops you"**, one growth line naming the binding limit and its key.
- **One word, "wants".**
- **The first Refinements raise limits** (pipes +10%, trading +10%) before they add +2% to a chain.

**From the economy designer:** the wonder sized at each stage from the Capital's income, and **Commission**, capped at
half a stage, as the Capital-wide sink.

**From lonely:**
- **Fund ▸**: pay for a friend's want after its window.
- **"While I'm away"**: an opt-in budget that funds the crew's wants that waited past their window. Ben came back to
  *"I let them down"* with 5.5M he could have given (ben d36).

**From wild:** **Widen** as a private sink that scales with income, on one condition (§2).

## 2 · What I reject, and why

**1. Gold never buys a want's materials** (outsider).
- Mia had no friend who made bricks. She wrote *"the gold feels empty"* (mia d15) and never played again.
- Ben is a phone skimmer. He won't build a two-seat chain for one Market Hall.
- The outsider's own "Cancel · your gold back after three play days" admits that wants can sit unfilled. With a window
  measured in sessions, friends keep their moment and nobody is walled.

**2. Gold at once, with no window** (lonely's "Buy now", wild's "Don't wait ▸" with a friend who could fill it).
- On day 21 Cleo held 500 bricks in her Storage and *"could not find how to hand Ana 100 bricks"* (cleo d21 #423).
- With Buy now, Ana pays 1,250 g, and Cleo's card never shows *"your Storage holds 500 · Send them ▸"*.
- The one felt chain of three runs is the thing this skips.
- Both buttons stay, but only where the window is zero.

**3. A deadline counted in hours** (psychologist: 12 hours; wild: 24 hours). Same reason as my dropped 6 hours: friends
come back every 2–7 days, so the House always wins.

**4. Widening grey pipes** (wild).
- The first pipe door, grey to copper, is a copper ingot a tile: copper plus coal, on two seats under dealt land. That
  is the run's best two-friend chain shape (cleo d14 #341–#368, outsider §2).
- Widening grey ×4 with gold skips it.
- So **Widen applies only to Copper and Steel links**: the friend's copper opens the door, then gold widens it.

**5. Capital lots, and a branch next to a friend, now** (wild).
- Run 4 would carry a station project, Landmark tiers, the Era wonder and endless lots: four shared goals, which is the
  card inflation the outsider warned about.
- Ana's lost friend is row 4's job ("Find your friends"), not a 2-days-of-income branch.
- Both come later, if run 4 shows Landmark tiers and Commission don't absorb the banks.

**6. A two-friend keystone "later"** (lonely). Answered below: only where two *active* residents hold the inputs, with
the House backstop and the 2-day pass-over, so it never waits on someone who isn't there.

## 3 · The three open questions, final

**1. Can gold buy a want's materials? Yes, after the friends' window, from the hub at 2.5 × base, at once.**
- **The window** ends when every lit player of the big station who can make the good, or holds it, has had one session
  since the want was raised. It lasts at most 72 hours, and it is zero when there is no such player.
- **During the window,** the want fills by itself from the Yard's spare and from Sends at 1.3.
- **The want shows once to each friend who can fill it:** *"Ana's Big Factory wants 100 bricks · your Storage holds 500
  · Send them ▸"*.
- **After the window:** *"Buy the rest ▸ 1,250 g"* for the owner, or *"Pay the rest for Ana ▸"* (Fund) for a crew mate
  or a neighbour.
  - The gold goes to the hub, never between players, and never between same-device accounts.
  - Bought goods count 25% toward shared credit.
  - A friend's Fund still says *"Ben's gold bought your bricks 💛"*: a chosen act gets a name.

**2. Do hub orders become buyers? Yes.**
- A buyer at your trading post, filled from your production as it passes: no Storage, no Deliver.
- Sized at 30 minutes of measured income, paying 2.0×.
- Shown on the card only once filled (psychologist).
- Personal: there is no Send into someone else's order.

**3. A two-friend keystone? Now**, from the Water Tower on.
- **Where:** where two active residents hold the land inputs of bricks (stone + coal) or copper ingots (copper + coal).
- **When a seat is away:** its input comes from the station's House at 1.4, uncapped. The 2-day pass-over stays.
- **Elsewhere:** today's rule.
- **Why:** water was the keystone in every station and needed nobody. The only "together 4" of three runs had exactly
  this shape.

## 5 · One mark for run 4 that would prove it wrong

**Friends hold the keys:** at least a third of the material units that filled wants (buildings, pipe tiers, the wonder,
the Landmark) come from other players, named at both ends, while the crew spends at least 20% of what it earns.

If the crew spends but under a third comes from players, the window was too short or Buy the rest too easy, and gold
has replaced the friends again.
