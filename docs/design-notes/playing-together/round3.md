# Round 3: three rival concepts, a test suite, and decisions (no open questions)

The owner's instruction: the result must be ONE FULL DESIGN with every question decided by the team. Do not hand
questions back to the owner. When something is uncertain, decide, say why, and say what would make you change it.

## The three rival concepts (from round 2)

- **A · Stations with a Yard** (champion: the player psychologist, r2-5-lessons.md; close relatives: r2-3-lonely.md
  "No empty tables", r2-6-outsider.md "Four Seats, a Yard and Honest Pipes"). Small places of ~4 seats around a
  shared Yard; you join the Yard by laying your own pipe into it; a fixed friend price; caretakers / lodgers.
- **B · Streets & Lines** (champion: the wild idea thrower, r2-1-wild.md). A crew of 1–6 lives on lots facing one
  corridor and shares a game-laid pipe (the Main, a many-ended teleporter, no merged networks); crew trade at a fixed
  price; the Main grows into a Line across the map; Lines link and rent capacity.
- **C · Places, Crews, Links** (champion: the friends-first designer, r2-2-friends.md). Three separate layers:
  stations are places (4 seats or 2-seat halts around a plaza), crews are people (1–8 who chose each other, may span
  places), links are bilateral trades between two companies that chose each other; the plaza only connects chosen
  pairs, never a shared network.

The economy designer's "Yards, Lines and the hub as the bank" (r2-4-economy.md) is the economic layer all three can use:
price levels, personal hub limits, the Port, trunk tolls, Houses, numbers scaled to typical income (≈800 g/min on day 1
to ≈4,900 g/min on day 28; income flat from day 14 to 21).

## Questions every concept must DECIDE (with the reason)

1. Do strangers who live in the same place share the shared thing (Yard / Main / plaza), or is it for a crew only?
2. Group size: fixed 4 seats, 2–6, elastic? What about couples, groups of 6–8, friends who fall out?
3. What exactly is shared: a Yard network, a Main (teleporter), or nothing but chosen bilateral links?
4. Direct trade only, or transit (trunk lines / Lines with tolls)? Who owns and maintains long pipes?
5. Prices: a fixed crew price, seller-chosen, or a ladder? How to stop gold funnelling to alts?
6. NPCs: what fills empty seats and what replaces a leaver's trades, how strong, how labelled, how to stop the
   "fake quitter" gold farm?
7. Absence: the exact timeline (resting / caretaker / packed), how long a seat is held, who may hold it.
8. Choosing a position: what the picker shows, the default for people who don't choose, moving later.
9. The testers' existing world: the migration, step by step (no wipe).
10. Estate size and the start area: is the start area (4 parcels, 16×16, with iron 3×3 and copper/coal/stone 2×2
    patches from the surveyors; 225 of 256 tiles stay free) too small or too complete? Decide: keep, enlarge, or move
    some patches into the first buyable parcels so there is a reason to expand.
11. The late game (week 3–4 and the next season): what do groups and the world do together?
12. Performance: no district-wide networks; what is simulated how.

## Hard cases (the test suite every concept must pass)

a. Three friends start tonight, a 4th joins on day 8, one quits on day 3 and returns on day 36.
b. A solo player on a server of 5 players; and a server shrinking from 200 to 20.
c. A newcomer in week 3 among veterans.
d. A griefer with two alt accounts who tries to block, exploit or funnel gold.
e. Two friends who fall out on day 10.
f. A group of 7 friends.
g. Week 4 and the next season.
h. The testers' world migrating to the new model.
