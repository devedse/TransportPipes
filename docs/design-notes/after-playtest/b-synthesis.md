# After round B: one direction, the questions it must decide, and round C

The moderator's synthesis of the six round-B papers.

## The direction most of you champion: "Needs from the land, gifts by pipe"

Five of six champion one combination: the economy designer's land with a visible act of giving. The wild idea thrower
and the psychologist champion the same parts, but differ on how gifts relate to the Yard.

- **Needs from the land.** Copper, coal, sand, wood and stone are dealt per station, with no free copies on other
  estates. Energy, iron and water stay everywhere, so the first hour works alone. Every seat's starter kit makes its
  seat's own good from minute one, so the seat card tells the truth.
- **One price where every Era-1 chain pays both sides** (economy: 1.3; others: 1.5).
- **Houses sell land goods at about 1.4**, so nobody ever stalls. The lonely guardian's floor sets how much: two
  buildings' need per good, plus one per 10 levels.
- **Gifts by pipe.** One verb, **Send ▸**, on the building panel: it lays the pipe and names the friend. The receiver's
  building reads "Runs on Ben's sand", and the gift is drawn at the receiver's building, the giver's gate and the return
  card. **Thanks** is the second verb.
- **Sketch:** a crew mate draws a fix on your estate, and you build it with one tap.
- **The stranger shares the crew's Yard.** On a small server a stranger is seated in the small station next door and
  joins the crew's Yard and project, while the crew's own seats stay closed. Mia would have helped build the
  Foundation on evening one.
- **The fix plan's honesty and clarity fixes ship underneath:** F1, F2, F5, F7 and F8, and most of F3–F4. This direction
  replaces most of F6 and changes F4's recipes.
- **Guards** (economy, lonely, friends):
  - goods sent into a storage are paid at the seller's hub price (that closes the Ask funnel, `Asks.cs:246–251`);
  - the stand-in's old price only for crew members with 10+ play days;
  - a new Send cuts a stranger's existing supply by at most 10% a week;
  - while a seat is held for a sleeper, its stand-in sells the seat's land good uncapped;
  - "no copies" ships only after the one-tap gate pipe passes its phone replay.

## The questions round C must decide

1. **Send and the Yard.**
   - (a) *On top:* the automatic Yard stays for everyone, and Send puts a named friend first and makes the gift visible
     (friends, economy, lonely, outsider).
   - (b) *Friends give, strangers trade:* inside a crew goods move only by a chosen Send, and the automatic Yard is for
     strangers and Houses (wild; the psychologist leans this way).

   The test is the outsider's: "Send real or not shown at all". If (a), is Send more than a label? If (b), what happens
   when friends don't tap: do they trade less than strangers?
2. **The price: 1.3 or 1.5**, and how bots of a human size (10–15 buildings) confirm it.
3. **Bids and requests.** Does the game post "Ana's Glassworks could use your sand · Send it" (psychologist), or is a
   receiver's need shown only on the giver's building panel (outsider: no new cards)?
4. **The stranger next door:** seated there by default (lonely, outsider, friends) or welcomed by a crew's one-tap
   invite, with a fallback after 3 days (psychologist)?
5. **The project:** a basket fed by Send and paying the player price for what it takes (economy), or the Works, a
   machine paying feeders a share of its margin (wild, for later weeks)?
6. **What else ships:**
   - crew research at 75% off (wild; economy says it's worth about 3 hours of a basic Lab);
   - tending with a job (psychologist, lonely; outsider: a daily chore);
   - "first-comer is the host" on an empty server (wild);
   - the content bug in three Era-2+ recipes that lose value at base prices (economy).
7. **The first-evening budget:** five nouns and two verbs (outsider). Which words go?

## Round C: each of you has a job

- **economy, the land and the money (spec):**
  - the deal per station (what each seat gets, ★ levels, how a district's seats cover the goods);
  - the kits, the price (decide 1.3 vs 1.5 with numbers), the Houses' land sales and the solo floor;
  - the funnel guards, and the project's pay;
  - how a light bot run confirms it.
  Worked numbers for Capital NW's four seats and for Mia's station.
- **friends, gifts by pipe (spec):** Send, Thanks, Sketch and the receiver's line, with exact screens and texts.
  Decide question 1 for your spec (on top, or friends-only), and say what a phone player taps.
- **wild, the rival answer (spec):** "friends give, strangers trade", specified as fully as friends' version, so the team
  can choose, with what happens when friends don't tap. Also "first-comer is the host" if you keep it.
- **psychologist, the story (walkthrough):** the run's 28 sessions replayed under the direction, in both answers to
  question 1. For each: what Ana, Ben, Cleo, Dev and Mia would have done, felt, and written in the group chat, minute by
  minute on evening one and day by day after. Say where giving is *felt*, not only announced.
- **lonely, the exam for players alone and players who stop:**
  - Mia on run 1's server with no free human seat, and Mia beside Jo;
  - a solo player in week 3 against the House floor;
  - Ben's 33 days away under the land deal;
  - an alt trying to farm the stand-in or a Send.
  Pass or fail each spec, with the fix.
- **outsider, the exam for the first evening, the phone and fun:** a new player's first 20 minutes and a skimmer on a
  phone under each spec. Count the nouns and verbs, and the cards and toasts. Is it fun, or a chore? Pass or fail each
  spec, with the fix.

Write `c-<you>.md` (about 2,000–3,500 words). The specs come first, so the examiners and the storyteller read them:
economy, friends and wild write theirs at once, and the others start from the round-B papers and refresh from the specs
when they land.
