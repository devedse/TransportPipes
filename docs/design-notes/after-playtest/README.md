# The design team after the playtest: the record

The six designers of v1.2 "Playing together" (the wild idea thrower, the friends-first designer, the guardian of lonely
players and leavers, the economy and long-game designer, the psychologist and game historian, and the outsider), resumed
with their memory of the design, judge their own design against the AI playtest `story1` and the fix panel's plan
(`docs/playtest-fixes.html`), because the owner expected the playtest to call for more of a redesign.

| Files | Round |
| --- | --- |
| `brief.md` | The brief |
| `a-*.md` | Round A: each alone, their verdict on their own design and their proposal |
| `b-*.md` | Round B: everyone read everyone and the fix plan; two or three rival directions |
| `c-*.md` | Round C: the directions specified and played through the playtest's story; the examiners' verdicts |
| `c-shared.md` | The design team's one shared idea, at the end of round C |
| `votes.md` | Both teams' elections of a representative |
| `dialogue.md` | The two representatives' discussion, turn by turn, and their joint statement |

**The outcome:** `docs/playtest-redesign.html`, the revised plan for the owner, built from the joint statement and the
record.
