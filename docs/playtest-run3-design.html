TransportPipes · after AI playtest run 3 · the six designers of v1.2, three rounds

Gold opens doors, friends fill them

You said the biggest find of run 3 was that at some point there's nothing to do with your gold, so no reason to grow, and asked the original design team for a solution. The six of them answered together: the game doesn't lack things to spend on; gold can't reach what limits growth. After week 1 every limit on income is a material (bricks, copper, steel) that only your own Storage can supply, or a pipe that carries 100 a minute. Their answer: every want becomes a buyer on the pipes that friends can fill, gold finishes the rest after the friends have had their turn, and the screens say what stops you and what a purchase will do before you click.

Why gold piled up

The game has an unlimited sink, building levels, which paid back in under an hour early on. Gold piled up anyway, because after week 1 everything that decides income is priced in something gold can't buy:

  • The pipe. A grey pipe carries 100 a minute per connection. Cleo's level-25 Coal Pit made 882 coal a minute and sold 100; her Copper Pipe click did nothing because its ingots had to come from her own Storage.
  • Materials. The Big Factory needs 100 bricks, the Market Hall 500, the Steel Mill 300, and only your own Storage can pay them. On dealt land they're made from two friends' goods: "I don't know how to hand you 100 bricks directly" (Cleo, day 21).
  • Spending that hurt. The Lab's Max took Cleo from 783 to 285 g/min; Dev called a 329k upgrade "a trap".
  • Points with nothing to buy. Cleo had 1.6M research points by day 21, more than all five eras' trees together.

So growth stopped around day 12–13: Ana stayed at 951 g/min and 13 buildings to day 36, and the crew held 95–100% of all it earned. The players offered each other gold four times and had no way to use it. They came back for a friend counting on them and a moving shared goal, never for gold.

The solution, in build order

Each step is a playable commit. Days are the designers' estimates for one AI developer.

1

Spending is safe, and one line says what stops you

2–3 days
  • A preview before every purchase, from the flow solver, in one sentence: "+105 g/min", "+0 g/min: this pipe carries 100 · Copper Pipe ▸", "−498 g/min: the Lab eats 29 of your iron ingots".
  • Max stops at the pipe's limit (built already, with fix 4).
  • Prices also as time: "Market Hall · 50 min of your income + 500 bricks".
  • "What stops you" on each producer's panel and the return card: the real limit, its key and its price. Once the key is in, "Max now pays: +X g/min ▸".
  • Undo for an hour. It refunds the price less what the purchase earned meanwhile, and takes back the XP it gave, so it can't be farmed.

Replaces Max's blind "+21 · 238k", idle reasons that blame energy, and the Invest line's "Max … for 13k" against 27.8M banked.

2

Wants

7–9 days · the analysts' row 7
  • A building, upgrade or pipe tier short of materials stands on the map as a scaffold that wants them ("Big Factory · wants 100 bricks · 40 in"). It runs on the game's existing Ask engine, and its goods go into the build, never a Storage.
  • It fills in this order: your own producers; a friend's Send, or one tap from their Storage ("Ana's Big Factory wants 100 bricks · your Storage holds 500 · Send them ▸"), named at both ends with Thanks; the station's spare through the Yard, by itself, even while friends sleep; an Order (step 4).
  • A want shows once, to a friend who's playing and can fill it. Never "unanswered", never a list of who didn't help. When nobody near makes the good, it shows the chain that would.
  • Cancel, your gold back, until it stands.

Replaces paying materials from your own Storage, "N in your storages", Deliver, and the Plan board's Ask form.

3

The pipe is a want

1–2 days

A pipe at its limit offers its next tier in place, as a want: a Copper Pipe wants a copper ingot a tile, and copper ingots are copper plus coal, usually two seats' land. So the pipe wall becomes the most natural gift between friends. Cleo's 28 ingots are about six minutes of one Smelter.

Replaces the silent Copper Pipe click.

Worked: Ana's Big Factory costs 1,000 g by order, the Market Hall 5,000 g. Why this shape: price can't protect a friend's goods (100 bricks at any price are a rounding error of Ana's 39.8M), and players return every 2–7 days, so a wait in hours always lands first. Counting the sessions of friends who still play gives them a turn without waiting on someone who stopped (Ben was away 33 days). After the window, a friend still wins the race: a Brickworks makes 12 bricks a minute, an order ships 100 at 0.3.

Replaces "buy now", "fund", "pay the rest" and "commission": one verb.

5

The Era wonder becomes the Capital's want

3–3.5 days
  • A buyer in every Yard round, after the station's project; each stage sized when it starts at 6 hours of the Capital's income (about 1.4M of goods on run 3's day 15), not once at world start for 60 g/min.
  • Half of each stage comes only by pipe; the other half is open to orders.
  • Each finished stage gives every seat in the Capital a bottleneck perk for the Era: grey pipes +50%, then Hub Orders +25%. Personal tiers pay hours of your own income.
  • When the last stage stands, the next Era starts early. Every card tells your part and how it ended.
  • It sits in the goal tracker with your part from the start (the analysts' row 5, folded in here).

Replaces the Wonders minigame, Deliver for the wonder, and "Era 1 ended without me knowing".

6

Research points have a use

3 days · the analysts' row 10
  • A Lab with nothing to research rests and eats nothing.
  • Refinements follow an Era's tree, one at a time, kept running while you're away, at 5k × 1.6ⁿ points: first the wall-raisers (pipes +10%, trading +10%, Hub Orders +5%), then +2% to one chain, each line capped at +50%.
  • Each applies to every resident of the station who runs that chain, named: "Bricks +6% · Cleo's research 💛".
7

Hub orders become buyers

1–2 days

A daily order is a buyer at your trading post for 24 hours, filled from what flows past: 30 minutes of income, 2× base, offered only to players who play, shown once filled, personal. Every land good gets an Era-1 want (the Warehouse and Cargo Depot want 100 planks each).

Replaces the Storage chore behind daily orders (none delivered after day 14 in run 3).

8

A stage that needs two friends

2 days · the analysts' row 8

From the Water Tower on, where two residents who both play when the stage starts each make one input of bricks (stone + coal) or copper ingots (copper + coal), and both are Level 6 or more (when the Brickworks and Smelter unlock), that good is the keystone, at 3 hours of one maker. An away seat's input comes from its House; the 2-day pass-over stays. Elsewhere, today's rule. Run 3's only "together 4" had exactly this shape.

Replaces water as the keystone in all four stations, which needed nobody.

9

Widen Copper and Steel pipes

1.5 days

A Copper or Steel link at its limit widens ×2 for 1 hour of your income, ×4 for 2 more hours. Grey links don't widen, so the first door past 100 a minute stays a friend's copper. The one private sink that grows with income.

Rules for every step
  • No sink is upkeep: nothing charges, decays or maintains a resting estate; every sink is a choice.
  • The return card shows at most one line about people (a friend's want, a gift, a Thanks) and one "what stops you" line; the rest folds.
  • A player back after a week sees one friend's waiting want he can order ("Ana's Steel Mill wants 120 bricks · Order the rest for Ana ▸"), even if he can't make the good: Ben's "I still can't help".

Later, only if run 4 or the bots show gold still piling up: the station's Landmark tiers (also if a station stands its Landmark before day 28, or crew diaries after day 21 name no moving shared goal), the Capital's lots, a branch estate next to a crew that plays (also for a player alone if her mark fails), and "While I'm away".

How this was made

The six designers of v1.2 (the wild idea thrower, the friends-first designer, the guardian of lonely players and leavers, the economy and long-game designer, the psychologist and game historian, and the outsider) were resumed with their memory of the design and the first redesign, and given run 3's evidence, the analysts' review and your question (docs/design-notes/after-run3/design-brief.md).

  • Round A (team-a-*.md): each alone, a diagnosis and a proposal. All six found the same cause from six angles.
  • Round B (team-b-*.md): each read all six and gave a final position.
  • Round C: the economy designer drafted the shared text (team-shared.md); each signed or objected (team-c-*.md). All six signed; the amendments above came from friends, lonely, the psychologist and the outsider.

The analysts' other rows still get built before run 4: finding your friends, the stranger beside a crew that plays, a running gift that stays running, the return card that folds, and crew chat.
