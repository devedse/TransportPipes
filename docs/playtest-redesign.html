TransportPipes · v1.2 · after the first AI playtest · the revised plan

Needs from the land, gifts by pipe

You expected more of a redesign, so the six designers of v1.2 came back and judged their own design against the playtest. They agree: the world layer held, and the together layer failed by design, not only by bugs. Their one shared idea was then argued against the fix panel's plan, turn by turn, by one representative of each team. Both signed one plan. This page is that plan.

Each seat holds what the others don'tCopper, coal, sand, wood and stone are dealt per station and never copied. Your starter kit makes your seat's good from minute one. A friend makes what you can't.
One price where both sides gain1.3 × base between players and for the project. Houses sell land goods at 1.4, so a player alone, or one whose friend has gone, is never stuck.
Send ▸, on top of the automatic YardOne tap names the friend, keeps them first and lays the pipe. Their building reads "Runs on Dev's coal 💛", and Thanks answers it.

0 · Three decisions for you

Both teams left these to you. Nothing past step 4 is built until you decide.

1. A new world on the test server The land deal changes what every estate holds, so step 5 starts a fresh world, and your estate there restarts. Steps 1–4 ship first on today's world, so you keep playing it until then. The run-1 snapshots stay for replays.

My advice: yes, at step 5.

2. Going on past the fix plan Steps 1–3 are the fix plan's part, about 8–10 days, and can ship alone. They make the game honest and readable, but they leave trade between friends near 0 by construction: every estate still mines its own copper, coal and stone.

My advice: go on. Every step is a playable commit, so you can stop after any of them.

3. A stranger seated next door to a crew On a small server, a player who comes alone is seated by default in the small station next to a crew's, sharing its Yard and project. It helps the lonely player most. Your friends live beside someone they didn't choose; any of them can close it to future strangers, and nobody living there is moved.

My advice: yes.

1 · Why the fix plan wasn't enough

The fix plan repairs what was built wrong: a Yard that paid nobody, dead ends that blamed their inputs, a project out of reach and screens that hid it all. It still ships, as steps 1 and 3. But it left three causes standing, and they are in the design.

Nobody needed a friend Every estate gets copper, coal and stone (GameWorld.Survey.cs:13), and every kit makes energy. The advisor tells everyone to mine their own: own mine and factory make +54 g/min, a neighbour's ore at 1.5 makes +10. With honest lacks, trade between friends comes to about 0, and a solo player would earn 106% of a crewed one.
There was no act In 36 days: 0 Asks, 0 links, 0 tends, 0 pins, and no good knowingly sent to a friend. The only way goods moved between friends was an automatic matcher. Dev asked "how do I send?" three times; Cleo wrote "Sending copper ingots will need a station".
The price paid nobody both ways At 1.75 × base, a Smelter on a neighbour's copper loses money. At 1.5, a Brickworks on bought stone and coal does. Once the land is dealt, two bought inputs are the normal case. Only at about 1.3 does every Era-1 chain pay its buyer and its seller.
The fix panel agrees "Point 1, redesign before run 2: I concede it. Nothing run 2 could show on today's land is worth a run." That is the fix panel's representative, its player researcher, in turn 2. Run 2 on today's land would spend about 1.3M tokens to measure a 0 again. So run 2 moves after the land deal.

Where they changed their minds
  • The wild idea thrower championed "friends give, strangers trade", and accepted that it lost. Ben stops on day 3, before any friend's building needs his sand, so under it his sand runs nobody's building.
  • The psychologist moved the team to protect a stranger's running trade as fully as a friend's: "The stranger is our most fragile player."
  • The fix panel's economy designer checked the 1.3 margins against buildings.yaml: "their numbers are right… My 1.5 assumed one bought input."
  • The fix panel's researcher conceded the redesign before run 2, the cost, and the stranger next door.
  • The design team's economy designer conceded the order: the money switches on with the land, not before. It also conceded a House floor that keeps friends needed in gold, run-2 marks that fail when nobody acts, and deferring Sketch.
  • The fix panel's cooperation designer won two points: no probation for a stranger the game seated, and gift marks on the map only for the two ends.

4 · The new rules

The land: needs come from it

WhatRule
On every estateEnergy, iron ore and water. Loady's first hour (Levels 1–4) works alone, as today
Dealt per stationCopper ore, coal, sand, wood and stone. A corner station's four seats get 2, 1, 1 and 1 of them; a small station's two seats get 3 and 2. Every station holds all five once, and no seat holds both bought inputs of one chain (copper with coal, or stone with coal)
Where they lieThe first good is rich (★5/★4) in the start area beside the HQ, the second in the first parcel you buy (Level 5)
No copiesNo more free copper, coal and stone patches on every estate. There is no beach without sand, and Lumber Camps need old-forest markers
KitsThe seat's own producer first, then moilies: about 100 g/min on day 1 on every seat (93–104)
Where a good is missingThe Build menu names who makes it and which House sells it; the Deposits overlay labels the station's goods by seat. No copies doesn't ship without this line

The money: one price, and a floor under everyone

The hub buysA House buysA friend, the projectA House sells land goodsA House sells energy, ironThe hub sells
1.01.15 × your hub price1.3 (the bots confirm it against 1.5)1.42.02.5
  • The House floor. A House fully feeds one building per land good, at any level. Beyond that, v1.2's 25% cap applies. A friend therefore matters more as an estate grows, and nobody's first chain ever waits for a human. A player alone should earn 85–92% of a crewed one, per building, and there is a trigger at each end of that band.
  • The station backstop. "Whenever no seat of your station will sell you a land good (its owner has been away 3+ days, has Blocked you or set 'not with' you, or the seat is held), the station's House or the hub sells you what that seat doesn't cover at 1.4, with no cap." No player is ever the only door to a good.
  • Guards:
    • units going into a Storage, and same-device trades, pay the seller's hub price (that closes a live funnel in GameWorld.Asks.cs:246–251);
    • a stand-in pays its sleeper's old price only to a crew mate with 10+ play days on another device;
    • a unit the project takes is paid once, at its price;
    • goods bought from a House count 25% toward the project;
    • NPCs never owe;
    • new gold from NPCs, the project's premium included, stays under 6% of faucets.

Send: the act of giving

The automatic Yard keeps carrying every resident's spare to every honest lack, friends included. So a friend who never taps still runs his friends' buildings, and that is the relief Ben needed. Send adds the name, the choice and a real priority. It is what Cleo and Dev asked for.

Nobody needs more than they getno button: "Cleo's Smelter has all the coal it needs"—
  • The Yard's order: contracts → usual partners' running trade → Sends → everyone else, pro rata → the project, Houses, storage, the hub.
  • Nobody's running trade is cut by someone else's Send or arrival, a friend's or a stranger's. A Send takes only new spare, new lack, and what was going to the hub, a House or the project. Today's matcher shares pro rata and would halve Mia's sales on the day Dev's coal arrives.
  • A Send never exceeds the receiver's honest lack. A Send to a sleeper passes to his stand-in.
  • Who can send: any resident to any resident's building or to the project, a stranger after the two gates have met. Never to a House. At most 6 Sends per giver.
  • Thanks: once a day per pair. It gives no gold, no XP and no counts.
  • Bids: "Ana's Glassworks could use your sand · Send ▸", once per need. It shows only to a player who is playing, never "unanswered", and between strangers only after their gates met.
  • "Make it yours: Send ▸": when your goods ran a friend's building by themselves, your card says so once, and offers the tap.
  • Privacy: the gate pennants and the pellets in the giver's colour show only to the two ends.
  • Never shown: a "stopped" line, ever.

The stranger next door, and the first-comer

  • On a small server (under 50 active companies), "Find me neighbours" seats a stranger in the small station next to a crew's. She shares the crew's Yard and project; the crew's own seats stay closed. There is no probation for a stranger the game seated, because a squatter there holds nothing the crew needs.
  • The first player alone on an empty server reads, once: "You're the first in Capital. The next player who comes alone will live next to you." There is no timer and no countdown.
  • "Today here": three public district events on the return card, from the second session.

The first evening

  • Five nouns and two verbs:
    • the nouns: crew, seat, House ⚙, your gate (G), the Water Tower;
    • the verbs: Send and Thanks.
    "Station", "Yard", "starter kit", Handshake, Sketch, Ask, bid and keystone wait.
  • The first screen: one move-in toast, then Loady. The achievement and the level card wait until after Loady's first step.
  • No drag on a phone. Loady's pipe step is a one-tap planner.
  • Loady's gift step teaches Send to the Water Tower. It counts for nothing in the measures, because AI players do what Loady says.

8 · What neither team knows yet

  • Whether people want to give when they can. Send could go unused. If run 2 has named flows but no chosen Send by day 10, the land works and the act doesn't. Then the bid gets stronger, not the rule.
  • How much AI players flatter the game. They read every panel, followed Loady to the letter, and rated fun 3 while stuck for five weeks. A pass in run 2 means "not refuted"; human testers are the real check. The bots follow the advisor perfectly, so they flatter trade and parity too.
  • Whether a missing good feels like a friend or a wall to a player alone, whether a crew welcomes the stranger next door, and whether 1.3 holds at high levels, where margins are thin.
  • What the replays can't check. Run 1's snapshots can't replay the land deal. So steps 1–4 are checked on today's world, and the land only by the bots and run 2.

What would change the plan

  • The light crew trades under 15% at 1.3: check the advisor's chain steps first, then try 1.25.
  • Run 2's diaries read a friend's absence as a loss: the House land price falls to 1.35.
  • Solo parity leaves its band: the floor's trigger at that end applies.
